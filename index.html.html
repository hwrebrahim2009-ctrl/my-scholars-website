Meet the Scholars

Explore the major works, specializations, and lasting contributions of eight scholars.

SCHOLAR 01

Imam Al-Nawawi

Died 676 AH / 1277 CE
Hadith & Jurisprudence

Major Works

Riyad al-Salihin Al-Arba'in al-Nawawiyya Al-Majmu' Sharh al-Muhadhdhab Sharh Sahih Muslim

Riyad al-Salihin means 'Gardens of the Righteous,' and Al-Arba'in al-Nawawiyya means 'The Forty Nawawi Hadiths.'

Core Subject Matter

Hadith collections and commentaries focusing on legal rulings, prophetic etiquette, character-building, and spiritual discipline.

Academic Specialization

A master Hafiz of Hadith, a leading jurist of the Shafi'i school, and a renowned ascetic.

Service to Islam & Legacy

Dedicated his life to scholarship, teaching, and authorship. His works became foundational guides for Muslims worldwide.

SCHOLAR 02

Ibn Qayyim al-Jawziyya

Died 751 AH / 1350 CE
Spirituality & Islamic Law

Major Works

Madarij al-Salikin Zad al-Ma'ad Al-Dawa wa al-Dawa I'lam al-Muwaqqi'in

These titles are commonly translated as The Ranks of the Divine Seekers, Provisions of the Hereafter, The Disease and the Cure, and Informing the Signatories.

Core Subject Matter

Prophetic biography, legal deduction, spiritual stations, legal rulings, and remedies for spiritual ailments.

Academic Specialization

A master scholar of Qur'anic exegesis, Hadith, Fiqh, legal theory, and Islamic spirituality.

Service to Islam & Legacy

Enriched Islamic literature with profound psychological and spiritual insights, connecting religious teachings with human nature.

SCHOLAR 03

Shaykh al-Islam Ibn Taymiyyah

Died 728 AH / 1328 CE
Creed & Scholarship

Major Works

Majmu' al-Fatawa Minhaj al-Sunna al-Nabawiyya Dar' Ta'arud al-'Aql wa al-Naql Al-'Aqidah al-Wasitiyya

These works address religious verdicts, theological debates, and Islamic creed.

Core Subject Matter

Refutation of philosophical and theological doubts, Islamic creed, Fiqh, Tafsir, and governance.

Academic Specialization

A polymath, reformer, and authority in Tafsir, Hadith, comparative jurisprudence, and philosophy.

Service to Islam & Legacy

Defended his understanding of Islamic orthodoxy through scholarship during a period of major political upheaval.

SCHOLAR 04

Imam Al-Bukhari

Died 256 AH / 870 CE
Hadith & Narration

Major Works

Al-Jami' al-Sahih Al-Adab al-Mufrad Al-Tarikh al-Kabir

Al-Jami' al-Sahih is commonly known as Sahih al-Bukhari.

Core Subject Matter

Compilation of Prophetic traditions, prophetic ethics, and evaluation of Hadith narrators.

Academic Specialization

Known as Amir al-Mu'minin fi al-Hadith and a master critic of narrations.

Service to Islam & Legacy

Al-Bukhari made an enduring contribution to preserving the Sunnah through rigorous authentication.

SCHOLAR 05

Ibn Kathir

Died 774 AH / 1373 CE
Qur'an & History

Major Works

Tafsir al-Qur'an al-Azim Al-Bidaya wa al-Nihaya Tabaqat al-Shafi'iyya

These works include his famous Qur'anic commentary and historical chronicle.

Core Subject Matter

Qur'anic exegesis using transmitted evidence and comprehensive historical writing.

Academic Specialization

A distinguished Qur'anic exegete, historian, jurist, and Hadith scholar.

Service to Islam & Legacy

His Tafsir remains an important reference, while Al-Bidaya wa al-Nihaya is a major historical chronicle.

SCHOLAR 06

Imam Al-Ghazali

Died 505 AH / 1111 CE
Ethics & Theology

Major Works

Ihya' Ulum al-Din Al-Mustasfa fi Ilm al-Usul Al-Munqidh min al-Dalal Tahafut al-Falasifa

Their English titles include The Revival of the Religious Sciences and The Deliverer from Error.

Core Subject Matter

Reviving religious awareness and ethics, examining philosophy, and developing legal theory.

Academic Specialization

Known as Hujjat al-Islam, or the Proof of Islam.

Service to Islam & Legacy

He connected outward religious practice with inward spiritual development.

SCHOLAR 07

Ibn Al-Jawzi

Died 597 AH / 1201 CE
Preaching & History

Major Works

Sayd al-Khatir Talbis Iblis Al-Mawdu'at Al-Muntazam Safwat al-Safwa

These works cover personal reflections, fabricated Hadiths, history, and biographies.

Core Subject Matter

Spiritual reflections, examination of fabricated Hadiths, and historical biographies.

Academic Specialization

An eloquent preacher, historian, prolific author, and Hadith critic.

Service to Islam & Legacy

He used his eloquence to inspire people and encourage better conduct.

SCHOLAR 08

Imam Al-Shafi'i

Died 204 AH / 820 CE
Islamic Jurisprudence

Major Works

Al-Risala Al-Umm Diwan al-Imam al-Shafi'i

These works address legal methodology, jurisprudence, and poetry.

Core Subject Matter

Systematic formulation of Islamic legal theory, comparative jurisprudence, and poetry.

Academic Specialization

A defender of the Sunnah and founder of an independent legal school.

Service to Islam & Legacy

He helped establish Usul al-Fiqh as a systematic discipline for deriving legal rulings.
